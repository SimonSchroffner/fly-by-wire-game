# Fly By Wire ✈️

**A 3D flying obstacle-course game you control with your phone.**

Your phone becomes the plane's stick. A big screen — a laptop, a TV, a
projector — shows a paper airplane gliding through clouds and rings, with a
QR code underneath. Scan it, hold your phone flat like you're about to throw
a paper airplane, and tilt it to steer. Fly through rings, dodge rocks and
pillars, and see how far you get before the world (which keeps speeding up)
catches you.

No app to install, no controller to plug in — just a browser on each screen.

## How to play

1. Open the game on a big screen (laptop, TV, monitor).
2. Scan the QR code shown on screen with your phone's camera.
3. Hold your phone flat, like a paper airplane, and press **Start**.
4. Tilt to steer — pitch and roll fly the plane, the world does the rest.
5. Fly through rings and around obstacles for as long as you can. Your score
   is the distance flown.

## Report a bug or request a feature

This repository is the home for player feedback:

- **Found a bug?** [Open an issue](../../issues/new) and describe what
  happened, what you expected instead, and your device/browser if it's a
  controls or display glitch (phone model + OS are especially useful for
  motion-control bugs).
- **Got an idea?** [Open a feature request](../../issues/new) — new
  obstacles, game modes, multiplayer ideas, accessibility options, anything.
- Before filing, a quick search of [existing issues](../../issues) helps
  avoid duplicates, but don't stress over it — a duplicate is easy to close.

## Tips for a good bug report

- What you were doing right before it happened.
- What you expected vs. what actually happened.
- Device + browser (e.g. "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome").
- A screenshot or short screen recording if it's visual.
